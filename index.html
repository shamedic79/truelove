VERIFICATION PROCESS

How official verification works

  1. Complete the official MR.BONE True Love application form.
  2. Submit collection photos as requested.
  3. After the preliminary review, collectors may be asked to provide photos of selected limited editions showing the corresponding serial numbers.
  4. The official MR.BONE team makes the final eligibility decision.

Unofficial collector aid: This tracker helps you organise and estimate your collection score. It is not the official application and does not determine True Love eligibility.
